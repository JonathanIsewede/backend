Frequently asked questions

What is your shipping policy?
We offer free express 2-day shipping on all domestic orders over $75. International orders typically arrive within 5-7 business days via DHL Express.
How does the 30-day money-back guarantee work?
If you are not 100% satisfied with your hardware within 30 days of receipt, simply initiate a return via your account or contact support for a prepaid return shipping label and full refund.
What warranty comes with LUMEN products?
All LUMEN hardware products come standard with a 2-year full manufacturer warranty covering defects, battery performance degrade, and structural craftsmanship.
Can I update or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders are processed immediately by our fulfillment center. You may request a cancellation within 1 hour of placing the order by reaching out to our team.
